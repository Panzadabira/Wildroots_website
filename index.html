Core Gameplay

Wildroots: Grove of the Last Guardian is a 2.5D Tower Defense game where you gather resources, build defenses, and protect the Heart of the Grove from endless waves of corrupted creatures.

  • Gather resources from the environment
  • Build structures and defenses around the Heart
  • Fight enemies in real-time combat
  • Survive increasingly intense enemy waves

Choose your playstyle between Warrior, Hunter, or Mage, each offering a different approach to combat and survival.

Mobile-First Design & Beta

This project is designed primarily with mobile devices in mind. The current Beta version is still in development, subject to changes and balancing, and serves to gather your extremely valuable feedback to shape the future of the game.

Current languages: Italian, English, French, Deutsch.

About the Developer

Wildroots: Grove of the Last Guardian is the first published project by PanzaLabs, an indie developer. The goal of this release is to learn, grow, and improve through real player feedback while building a game driven by passion, experimentation, and persistence.
