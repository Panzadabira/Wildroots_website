Core Gameplay

Wildroots: Grove of the Last Guardian is a 2.5D roguelike tower defense where you gather resources, build your village and its defenses, and protect the Heart of the Grove. You are the Guardian, a Wandering Seed: every run is a new world, and the waves alternate calm, growth, assault and boss phases, until the Heart falls.

Ways to Play

Learn the ropes, survive for as long as you can, or take the fight to the forest together.

The Awakening

A guided tutorial for new Guardians.

Ancient Forest

Classic survival: endless cycles of phases and bosses, with no fixed end.

Thorn Storm

A short, high-intensity Rush mode with boosted XP.

Co-op

Two Guardians, one shared Heart, each with their own economy.

Play together, or not. Invite a friend with a private room code, or team up with an AI companion, even offline. Co-op unlocks at account level 5.

Build & Defend

One seed, one building. Discover ancient seeds hidden in the forest and grow them with experience to unlock new structures. Place them with a tap: they rise from the soil around the Heart.

Defend the Heart with the Defensive Briar (a heavy strike against ground enemies), the Thornspitter (long-range anti-air), the Rotten Puffball (a cloud of spores) and Treacherous Thorns (a decoy trap that lures even flying enemies).

Keep the village alive too: Mines and Mushroom fields produce resources, the Doctor heals the Heart, and the Merchant trades with you. Upgrade your hero and every defense with cards.

The Corrupted

Roots, Beasts, Rootcasters, Winged Ones that strike from the sky, Golems, and the Giants that close each cycle as bosses. A boss phase lasts until the boss is dead.

Explore & Discover

The world is generated from a seed and changes every run: ancient seeds guarded by sentinels, rare drops and temporary boosts, and a relic hidden somewhere in the forest, with no clue on the map. Use the big map to find your way and travel back to the Heart with a tap.

Keep track of everything in the Codex and the achievements, climb the leaderboards (Classic, Rush, per class, and Co-op), and take part in seasonal events. Some secrets are not on any list...

Your Companion

A cat guides you through the menu and tells the story, with cosmetic skins to collect. In the field, allied helpers such as the Gatherer and the Doctor lend a hand to the Heart.

Fair Play

Wildroots is free to play and not pay-to-win. Purchases are limited to cosmetic skins, convenience and in-game currency, with no random loot boxes. Optional rewarded videos give you extra Glabox, and ads are shown only after your consent.

Mobile-First Design
