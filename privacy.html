Privacy Policy for Wildroots: Grove of the Last Guardian

Last Updated: August 24, 2026

PanzaLabs ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile game, Wildroots: Grove of the Last Guardian (the "Game").

1. Information We Collect

We intentionally limit the data we collect to provide a seamless and safe gaming experience.

  • Automatically Collected Data: We use Unity Analytics and Unity Cloud Diagnostics to collect anonymous telemetry data (e.g., device model, operating system version, and crash logs) to optimize performance and fix bugs.
  • Google Play Games Services: If you choose to sign in using Google Play Games Services to access Cloud Saves and Leaderboards, we receive authentication tokens and your basic Google Play gamer profile (such as your Gamer ID and username). We do not have access to your personal Google account details (like your private email address or name).
  • In-App Purchases: The Game allows you to purchase virtual currency ("Glabox") via Unity IAP. All payment processing is handled securely by Google Play Billing. We do not collect, process, or store your credit card information or billing details.

2. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used exclusively to:

  • Save your progression, unlocked classes, and achievements across devices.
  • Display your Gamer ID on the global leaderboards.
  • Identify and resolve technical issues or crashes.
  • Prevent fraudulent activities or exploits within the Game.

3. Loot Boxes & Monetization

Wildroots features a transparent progression system. We do not employ random-reward monetization mechanics (e.g., "Loot Boxes" or "Gacha"). All in-app purchases grant specific, disclosed amounts of in-game currency or items.

4. Third-Party Services

The Game utilizes the following third-party services, which may collect data as outlined in their respective Privacy Policies:

  • Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity Technologies: https://unity3d.com/legal/privacy-policy

5. Children's Privacy

Our Game does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable legal age in your region). If you are a parent or guardian and believe we have inadvertently collected personal data from your child, please contact us so we can delete the information.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect changes in our services or legal obligations. We will notify players of significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
