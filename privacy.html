Privacy Policy for Wildroots: Roguelike TD

Last Updated: November 16, 2026

PanzaLabs is the trade name of Jonathan Barenco, an individual developer based in Switzerland ("we", "us", or "our"), who is the controller of your data when you play our mobile game Wildroots: Roguelike TD (subtitle: Grove of the Last Guardian) (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, why, who receives it, and how you can control it. You can reach us at panzadabira@gmail.com.

See also our Terms of Service.

1. Who the Game is for

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. In some countries the age at which you can consent to the processing of your data on your own is higher (up to 16): if that applies to you, ask a parent or guardian before accepting personalized ads or creating an account. If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to us and we will delete it.

2. Information We Collect

We keep the data we collect to what the Game needs to run.

  • Player ID (always): the first time you open the Game, Unity Gaming Services creates an anonymous player ID and stores a session token on your device. This lets you save your progress in the cloud, even as a guest.
  • Game progress: your level, experience, virtual currency, unlocks, statistics and settings. They are stored on your device and, when you are online, in Unity Cloud Save.
  • Account (optional): you can protect your progress with a nickname and password, or link Google Play Games. For Google Play Games we receive an authentication code and your basic gamer profile (Gamer ID and username), not your private email address.
  • Player name and leaderboards: the name you choose, your scores, your level, your class, the title you have equipped and the date are shown publicly on the global leaderboards. Do not use your real name if you do not want it to be public.
  • Online co-op (only if you use it): when you create or join a co-op room, Unity Relay and Unity Lobby (part of Unity Gaming Services) handle the connection. The room is private and is joined with a friend code. Your player name and class are shared with your partner, and game data (such as your position, actions and the preset emotes and signals you send) is exchanged during the match. There is no text or voice chat, and we do not record matches. The Game connects you through Unity's relay servers, so players do not exchange IP addresses directly; Unity receives your IP address to provide the service. The offline AI companion runs on your device and sends no data.
  • Game configuration: when you open the Game, it downloads settings from Unity Remote Config (for example, whether a time-limited reward is available). This uses your anonymous player ID and your IP address, and it does not depend on your choice about usage statistics.
  • In-app purchases: purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We receive confirmation that a purchase was completed. We never see or store your card or billing details.
  • Advertising data: if you watch rewarded video ads, our advertising partners (section 5) may process your advertising ID, IP address and device information (model, operating system, language, country).
  • Usage statistics (only if you agree): anonymous gameplay events, such as when you start or end a run (with your class, difficulty, run length and score), whether you watch a rewarded video, and whether you link an account, plus technical data such as device model, operating system version, language, approximate country (from your IP address) and crash or performance information. They are linked to a random installation ID and, if you are signed in, to your Unity player ID, never to your name.
  • Technical data: like any online service, Unity Gaming Services, Google Play and GitHub (used to check for new versions) receive your IP address when the Game connects to them.

3. How We Use Your Information and Our Legal Basis

  • Run the Game, save your progress and show leaderboards (contract): player ID, progress, account, player name, scores and game configuration.
  • Let you play online co-op with a friend (contract): player name, class and game data exchanged with your partner through Unity Relay and Lobby. Co-op room data is kept only while the room exists.
  • Process purchases and give you what you bought (contract): purchase confirmation from Google Play.
  • Show personalized rewarded ads (your consent): we ask you at first launch, and you can change your choice at any time (section 6). If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalized.
  • Improve the Game with anonymous usage statistics (your consent): we ask you after the ads choice at first launch, and you can change your choice at any time (section 6). If you decline, no statistics are collected.
  • Keep the Game secure and fair (legitimate interest): prevent cheating, exploits and abuse of the leaderboards, and fix technical problems.
  • Comply with the law (legal obligation), where applicable.

4. Loot Boxes & Monetization

Wildroots does not use random-reward monetization (no "Loot Boxes" or "Gacha"). Every in-app purchase gives a specific, disclosed amount of virtual currency or items. You can also earn virtual currency by watching optional rewarded videos, with a daily limit.

5. Who Receives Your Data

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the providers that make the Game work, which act on our behalf or under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services: Authentication, Cloud Save, Leaderboards, Remote Config, Relay and Lobby for online co-op; Unity Analytics for usage statistics; Unity IAP; and Unity LevelPlay with Unity Ads for rewarded ads): unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google (Google Play Games Services, Google Play Billing, Google Play): policies.google.com/privacy
  • GitHub: the Game downloads a small public file from GitHub to check whether a new version is available. GitHub can see your IP address when this happens.

Some of these providers process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, they rely on legal safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

6. Your Choices

  • Ads and statistics consent: in the Game, open Account → Privacy settings to accept or decline personalized ads and anonymous usage statistics at any time. You can also reset your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Guest or account: you can play as a guest, or link a nickname/password or Google Play Games to recover your progress on a new device.
  • Rights: depending on where you live (GDPR and UK GDPR in Europe, the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection) you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of your data, to receive a copy of it, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Write to us to exercise them. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Delete Your Account and Data

You can delete your account and data at any time:

  • In the Game: open Account → Delete account and confirm. You need to be online. This permanently deletes your Cloud Save data and your account in Unity Gaming Services, sends a request to delete your usage statistics, and erases the progress stored on your device. It cannot be undone.
  • By email: write to panzadabira@gmail.com from the address you use for the Game and tell us your player name. We will reply within 30 days. If your request is complex, or we receive many requests, we may extend this period by up to 60 more days (90 days in total), and we will tell you why within the first 30 days.

When you delete your account in the Game, your player name on the leaderboards is replaced by a neutral name before the account is removed. Your scores may remain on the leaderboards, without any link to your account or to your previous name. We may also reset the leaderboards from time to time. Purchase records kept by Google Play are managed by Google, not by us.

8. How Long We Keep Data

We keep your player ID, progress and account for as long as you use the Game. When you delete your account, we remove it as described in section 7. Usage statistics are kept for up to 13 months. Technical data kept by our providers (such as IP addresses in server logs) follows their own retention periods.

9. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when the Game or the law changes. We will update the "Last Updated" date above. If a change affects how we use your data in a significant way, we will tell you in the Game.
